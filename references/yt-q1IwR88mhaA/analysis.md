## 1. Why it works (the formula, in one screen)
1. **Intro = 42 s of montage at ~1 shot / 1.5 s**, then the pace drops to a calm tutorial. Fast → slow is the trick:
   the first 40 s earns the viewer's patience for 13 minutes of screen recording.
2. **Every intro sentence gets its own picture, and the picture is a literal visual pun of the words**
   ("so many options" → many blob characters popping in; "complexity" → a stopwatch named COMPLEXITY that
   spins away; "while you sleep" → a man asleep; "beginner friendly" → huge yellow glowing words behind the host).
3. **Title card at second 1**, not later: "THE COMPLETE / GROK BOT / TUTORIAL" builds word by word on a dark
   textured wall, the O of TUTORIAL is replaced by the product mascot (a red blob), then a cartoon pointing finger
   punches the camera. Promise first, person second.
4. **Name lower-third types on letter by letter** ("C… CHARLIE", yellow italic caps) on the first talking-head shot.
5. **Body = one continuous real demo**: the real app full screen + round face-cam bubble bottom-right, the host
   does the thing live (create bot → settings → connect Gmail → watch its computer → teach → routines →
   marketplace → group chat). Each chapter ends with a result on screen (bot answers, Amazon shortlist, routine set).
6. **Cutaways only where the words are abstract**: talking head full frame to explain a concept, a dark hex-tile
   diagram (logos → hub → app grid) for "how it works", big caption bar "ALL OF YOUR BOTS SHARE ONE CLOUD COMPUTER"
   for the key fact, a "Finance bot" icon + italic description overlay for each bot type in the list.
7. **Chapter changes have no chapter cards**: a whip-pan / spin blur (≈0.3 s) jumps from screen to host or to a
   new screen. Chapters live in the YouTube chapter list, not on screen.
8. **Ending**: summary in talking head → "LINKS BELOW" yellow italic → like-thumb + red SUBSCRIBE sticker → peace
   sign → end screen (white, "LATEST VIDEOS" / "SUBSCRIBE" slots).

## 8. Template for our videos (what to change in the old pipeline)
1. **Cold open (0–1 s)**: one line of narration that names the promise ("If you want X, this is the video").
2. **Title build (1–4 s)**: 3-line kinetic title, the product mark/mascot replaces one letter; SFX pop per word.
3. **Pointing / "you" beat (4–5 s)**: a graphic that addresses the viewer.
4. **TOC montage (5–40 s)**: narration "by the end you'll know A, B, C, D, E" → 1 picture per item, 1.5–2.5 s each,
   pop SFX on each; return to the anchor shot (our mascot / bot mark set) every ~10 s.
5. **Key claim slam (≈25 s)**: 1–2 giant words behind the anchor ("BEGINNER FRIENDLY").
6. **Body**: chapters that each do one real task and end on its result; screen sections held long (8–20 s) with
   zoom to the active area + arrow sticker; concept sections cut every 4–6 s with diagrams; no music under the demo.
7. **List section**: icon + name + italic one-line description per item (Finance bot…).
8. **Key-fact caption bar** once or twice (white bold caps, bottom).
9. **Outro**: recap → CTA text ("LINKS BELOW") → like/subscribe stickers → end-screen slots.
Chapter changes: a 0.3 s whip/spin/zoom blur + whoosh, no chapter cards (they are in the YouTube description).
