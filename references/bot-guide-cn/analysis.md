## Beats with timestamps (approx.) and what was said (translated from the subtitles)
- 0:00 Title. "This episode I'll take you from zero." Chips highlight chapters as they come.
- 0:50 ZERO TO ONE: from zero you can build a one-person company. Start: zero-base beginner → watch this episode → follow step by step.
- 1:10 You can skip coding but you need an assistant. AI Agent evolution: before = chat box ("you ask, it answers"); later = coding agent (writes code, high barrier); now = Personal Agent (schedules tools to do the work) — "a brand-new solution".
- 1:50 Install ("old school: official website"): open the official site → download. Slogan on screen: "Your all-day Bot team; real work all handed to them." "It really isn't hype."
- 2:00 First Bot, screen recording: "① New ② Pick an avatar ③ Name ④ Set rules." The Bot then asks you questions. Your account is bottom-left.
- 3:00 Directory of ready-made Bots and connectors: "e.g. connect Notion"; marketing, design, hiring, operations "a big pile".
- 3:20 Multi-device illustration; "this may be a key reason I hype it".
- 3:30 Real case "let the Bot make money for me": a fixed script on my Windows PC for cutting novel-promo videos: ① cut videos ② publish ③ fill links back. Bot trims the Windows desktop assets into video, runs daily around 17:30, fills links back when done. Also covers for comic-drama.
- 4:30 Devices: before Grok Bot nothing connected; now "all my phones and computers have no walls between them"; other agents at most had prototypes.
- 5:00 "My company: three real business departments." One Bot, one job; job description = Skill. Topic Bot pushes viral topics daily; Bot finds editing material; forms an editing project.
- 5:50 Roles hand off: pick topic → write → design → edit. Each role = a mature Skill = employee handbook. Design Bot makes the cover directly, adapted to every size. Design Skill is open source; the presenter wrote detailed beginner docs.
- 6:30 "05 A real one-person company": comic-drama department list: chief, editor, copywriter, publisher, assets, each with a status ("Sent 1 video", "6 scripts ready", "Locked: not allowed to run", "Standing by").
- 6:50 Why Personal Agent is the new way out (for Codex/WorkBuddy-style agents): before = strong single tasks in the cloud; now = lands in your own business. "Not who is strongest at one thing, but who can finish the job."
- 7:10 THE KEY: scheduling. "Some say its code isn't top-tier and images aren't best." Boss view: you don't need the best at everything, just orchestrate.
- 7:20 ALL FOR YOU: computer, phone, work software → your business.
- 7:30 06 Quick-launch tips: two ways (side button / voice "Hey Grok"). Side button needs Grok Bot ≥ 1.16 and iPhone 15 Pro or later (Action button). Steps in Shortcuts ("Start Voice Chat with Bot"), also Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap (double/triple tap). Voice: say "hey grok" three times to record. Permissions: calendar, reminders, alarms.
- 8:30 Visual chat UI "run the team like a strategist".
- 8:40 07 Pitfalls: Issue 01 it over-corrects (big gaps between sentences; small edits, takes lines) → my fix: let a coding tool change code, or use a tuned script. Issue 02 no per-Bot window yet (feedback sent to the vendor, hope soon). Biggest trap: over-designing the org chart while the business itself is vertical.
- 9:50 ROI: be rational, compute efficiency first (invest: subscription + break-in time; return: time saved, results).
- 10:00 Why I love it: "On Codex alone I've used 60+ billion tokens" (usage heat map). Before: strong dev, bloated use (new chats, archive, delete). After: minimal (minimal interaction → digital company architecture → driven by business). Water theory: swapping a barrel of water for a case of bottled water; to form a habit, shorten the chain: have an idea → call anywhere → hand off. "The smoother, the more you use it." Works on business trips, even on a beach.
- 11:00 The dream: team work like a strategist commanding thousands of miles away; "even a mediocre talent can be Zhuge Liang". Ends on a calligraphy title card.
