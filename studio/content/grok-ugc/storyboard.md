# Storyboard — Grok Bot + UGC ad factory (4 parts)

Idea: a little factory line; every part moves one 'ad token' further along it (find → break down → recreate → multiply → launch).
Look: light pastel backgrounds rotating per shot, white cards, dark Grok Bot windows (BotGuideReal kit), coloured bot marks
(pink = AI UGC Studio, teal = Ads Manager). AI 'creators' are drawn avatars in phone frames, never photo faces.
Hook follows the standard template (yt-q1IwR88mhaA §8): payoff first, claim slam, 5-item TOC montage, open loop.
**[R]** = real screenshot with source tag (4 shots). No captions. Sound: whoosh per shot, pop on every TOC picture, typing on prompts, ding on results.
