# Storyboard — "The lazy way" (light version, review before build)

Idea: one small offer travels from "pick a niche" to "a client who keeps buying agents"; the picture is a ladder of money-in tiles that grows.
Look: ember theme. All CODE-ONLY (no presenter, no footage). [E] = real evidence screenshot to capture after your OK (public pages, with source tag).

1. hook: dark title, a sleepy Bot mark on a hammock drifts, "$" tiles pop into a jar (illustration)
2. lazy: chapter card "THE LAZY WAY" + a long road turning into a short straight line
3. step1: browser window, a one-page site builds block by block (hero, button, footer) [E: page-builder site]
4. niche: five niche cards (gym, restaurant, garden, windows, pipes) flick past and one lifts
5. know: org-style card "my dad's landscaping company" with a lightbulb, three "I know this" chips
6. pains: split card, a mower icon; four pain rows appear (reviews, phone, website, leads)
7. portfolio: phone screen, empty gallery fills with lawn-stripe photos (drawn)
8. plumber: map card with a pin; checklist ticks: Google profile? website? ads? [E: map listing with no website]
9. columns: 3-column table fills row by row (niche | pain | help)
10. not-every: before/after cards, a "reply bot" crossed out for the plumber
11. need: invoice and accounts doc slides in, highlighted "real need"
12. recurring: calendar flips months, coin drops each month, big "monthly" count-up
13. bridge: arrow from the table to a "how do I get customers?" bubble

## Part 3 — Website (13)
1. hook: chapter card with a 7-day strip
2. scrape: map with many pins, no-website ones glow, counter to 500-600
3. filter: list narrows to rows with phone + reviews + hours, no site
4. choice: two bars: calling = 2 days, email = 1 day
5. email: mail composer types the offer, price tag $499 pops
6. trick: a mailbox; a single reply lights, the rest stay dim (the site is not built yet)
7. reply: chat view, three short questions and a photo upload
8. build: browser window builds the one-page site, Bot beam sweeps
9. fear: 500 tiny site thumbnails dissolve to a handful (only replies get one)
10. math: tiles 500 emails -> 40-50 replies -> 20 buy, then "20 x $500 = $10,000" (label: example)
11. work: credits meter drains a little, then a map of towns lights up
12. saturated: crowded shelf of site cards, a "no" counter ticks up, becomes data points
13. goal: progress bar fills to 20-30 sold

5. reviews: review stars, a reply types itself
6. keep: ladder of five tiles stacks up with a monthly coin on each
7. restaurants: restaurant illustration, a free review-agent gift box opens
8. agents: three agent windows (stock, bookings, finance) with small charts
9. test: sample data goes in, answers come out, a human check mark
10. foot: foot-in-door drawing, door ajar, a new offer slips in
11. recap: 5-step path lights up in order
12. harder: Bot crew silhouette, a big "full agency" sign far on the horizon
13. outro: closing card "one niche, one page, one offer"
