# English script — Bot Beginner's Guide (2:27)
Narration is also the on-screen caption. Edit `src/slides.json`, then run `python tts.py` and `npx remotion render src/index.ts Guide out/guide-en.mp4`.

## 01 · intro · 0:00

Welcome. In the next few minutes, I'll walk you through setting up your very first Bot employee, step by step.

## 02 · what · 0:09

This is not another chat box, and it's not a coding agent either. Think of it as a truly smart assistant.

## 03 · first · 0:18

Getting started takes four steps. Create a new Bot, pick an avatar, give it a name, and set its rules. I named my first one Translator.

## 04 · job · 0:32

Every Bot gets one job. The job description is simply a skill. For example, a topic picker that sends me the best ideas every morning.

## 05 · handoff · 0:44

Jobs can hand work to each other. Pick a topic, write, design, then edit. Each role is a mature skill, like an employee handbook. Use it first, add rules, add limits, and it gets sharper every time.

## 06 · manager · 1:04

For important departments, you can assign a manager. You can't direct every employee yourself, so scheduling is the key.

## 07 · devices · 1:14

The same Bot works across all your devices. Log in on your phone, and one sentence is all it takes.

## 08 · launch · 1:23

There are two quick ways to call it. Bind it to your side button, or just say, Hey Bot.

## 09 · pitfalls · 1:32

A few pitfalls to watch for. Bots tend to over-correct. There's no separate window for each Bot yet. And the biggest trap of all: spending more time designing roles than doing real work.

## 10 · roi · 1:47

Be rational, and do the math first. You invest a subscription and some setup time. You should get back saved time and real results. Otherwise, it's meaningless.

## 11 · after · 2:02

Before, powerful tools felt bloated. Now it's minimal. Simple interaction, a digital company structure, and everything driven by your real business.

## 12 · outro · 2:16

So start small. One Bot, one real task. Build everything around your real business, and let it work for you.
